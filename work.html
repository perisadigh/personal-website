Strength training appMade for someone

Strength training app

I’d spent a lot of time learning about strength training, so when my dad wanted to start, I built him an app to help him get going without having to figure it all out from scratch.

It creates weekly training plans, selects exercises to cover different muscle groups, and sets targets for progressive overload. It draws from a database of exercises, each with its own rating.

Built with Claude Code and Supabase.

Job search toolMade for me

Job search tool

I got tired of looking through job postings that weren’t relevant to me, so I built something to do it for me.

It scans job listings and filters them against my background, what I’m looking for, and requirements like location and visa sponsorship.

So far, it’s scanned over 80,000 jobs.
