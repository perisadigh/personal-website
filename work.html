1Made for someone

Strength training app

I built a strength training app for my dad after spending far too much time researching how to put together a good workout programme.

It creates weekly training plans, selects exercises based on how different muscles are trained, and sets progressive overload targets. It has a database of 127 exercises, each with its own rating.

Built with Claude Code and Supabase.

2Made for me

Job search tool

I got tired of looking through job postings that weren’t relevant to me, so I built something to do it for me.

It scans job listings and filters them against my background, what I’m looking for, and requirements like location and visa sponsorship.

So far, it’s scanned over 80,000 jobs.
